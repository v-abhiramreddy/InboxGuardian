Inbox Guardian

AI-powered phishing and scam detection for your Gmail inbox

What Inbox Guardian does

Scans your Gmail inbox for phishing, scams, spam, and suspicious emails in real time Uses rule-based heuristics, a trained ML classifier, and Google Gemini AI

Scores each email 0–100 and explains why it is or isn't a threat Plain-language explanations — no technical jargon

Read-only access — never sends, deletes, or modifies your emails Uses gmail.readonly scope only. You can revoke access anytime.

Works for any Gmail user — institutional, college, and platform emails supported Recognises legitimate mailing lists, .edu, .gov, and .ac.in domains correctly

Note: Sign-in is currently limited to approved test accounts while our Google OAuth app is under verification. Contact us to be added, or try Demo Mode — no sign-in needed.
