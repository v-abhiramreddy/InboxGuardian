2. What Inbox Guardian Does

Inbox Guardian is an AI-powered email security tool that reads your Gmail inbox (with your explicit permission) and analyzes emails for phishing, scam, and spam risk using:

  • A rule-based heuristic scoring engine
  • A machine-learning classifier trained on public phishing and spam datasets
  • Google Gemini LLM for plain-language threat explanation

The Service provides risk scores and explanations to help you make informed decisions about your email. It does not take any action on your email on your behalf.

3. Google Account & OAuth Access

To use the full analysis features, you authorize Inbox Guardian to access your Gmail account using the gmail.readonly OAuth scope. This grants strictly read-only access. The Service cannot and will never:

  • Access any Google service outside of Gmail message reading

You can revoke this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access does not delete any cached analysis data — contact us separately for that.

5. No Warranty — Accuracy Limitations

Important: Inbox Guardian is a security assistance tool, not a guaranteed security solution. Its risk scores and classifications are probabilistic — they will sometimes be wrong.

Specifically:

  • False negatives: Some phishing, scam, or spam emails may be classified as safe. Do not rely solely on Inbox Guardian to identify all threats in your inbox.
  • False positives: Some legitimate emails (particularly from organizations with non-standard mail server configurations) may be incorrectly flagged as suspicious.
  • AI analysis: Gemini-generated explanations reflect probabilistic AI reasoning and may contain errors or mischaracterizations of specific emails.
