Overview

Inbox Guardian is an AI-powered email security tool that scans your Gmail inbox for phishing, scams, and spam using a combination of rule-based heuristics, a machine-learning classifier, and Google Gemini LLM analysis.

This policy explains exactly what data Inbox Guardian accesses, what it stores, and what it never touches. We have written it to be direct and specific — not filled with legal boilerplate.

Gmail Access

Inbox Guardian requests one Gmail OAuth scope:

gmail.readonly — read-only access to your Gmail messages and metadata. This scope does not permit sending, drafting, deleting, modifying, or inserting messages of any kind.

This access is used solely to fetch email content (sender, subject, headers, body text, and links) for on-the-fly threat analysis. Emails are fetched at the moment you open the dashboard and are not retained after your session ends, except as described in the storage section below.

What Inbox Guardian stores

Three files may be written to disk during a session. Here is exactly what each one contains:

File What is stored Email body stored?
llm_cache.json Gemini's plain-language explanation string, keyed by a hash of: email ID prefix, risk score, subject line, and prompt version. No email body is hashed or stored. No
audit-log.jsonl Timestamp, email ID, risk score, category, and confidence. No sender, subject, or body. No
results.json Email ID, risk score, category, confidence, Gemini explanation, subject line, and sender address. No email body or full message content. No
Email body content is never written to disk. It is held in memory only for the duration of analysis (typically under 2 seconds per email) and discarded immediately after the risk score is computed.

What Inbox Guardian never does

We want to be explicit about the boundaries of this application:

Action Status
Send, draft, delete, or modify your emails Never
Store your email body content on any server Never
Share your email data with third parties Never
Use your email data to train any model Never
Sell or monetize your data in any form Never
Access emails outside of your active session Never

Third-party services

Inbox Guardian uses the following external services during analysis:

Service Purpose Data sent
Google Gemini API Generating plain-language threat explanations for high-risk emails Sender, subject, truncated body text (max 2,000 chars), link list, auth header results. Never your full inbox or account credentials.
Gmail API (Google) Fetching your email messages for analysis OAuth token used server-side only. Never exposed to the UI or logs.
OpenPhish feed Live phishing URL threat intelligence No user data sent. Feed is downloaded and cached locally (6-hour TTL). URL lookups happen locally against this cache.

Both Google services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Inbox Guardian's use of Gmail data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

OAuth token handling

Your OAuth token (which grants temporary access to your Gmail) is stored locally in token.json on the server for the duration of your session to avoid requiring repeated sign-ins. This file is never logged, transmitted, or exposed through the application interface. You can revoke Inbox Guardian's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Demo mode

Inbox Guardian includes a demo mode (?demo=1) that uses pre-loaded sample data — no Google sign-in or Gmail access is required. No personal data of any kind is accessed or stored when using demo mode.

Children's privacy

Inbox Guardian is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date at the top and, where practical, notify users via the application. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
