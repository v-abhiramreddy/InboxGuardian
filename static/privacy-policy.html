INBOX GUARDIAN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026 · Team Sentinel

🔒 Inbox Guardian reads your Gmail inbox solely to detect phishing and fraud threats. We do not store, sell, or share your email content with anyone.

1. What We Access

Inbox Guardian authenticates via Google OAuth 2.0 using a single, read-only scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly.

This scope allows us to read email metadata (sender, subject) and message bodies in order to perform threat analysis. It does not allow us to send, delete, or modify any emails or Gmail settings.

2. What We Collect & Store

  • Email content — processed in memory during your session only. Never written to disk or any database.
  • Analysis results (results.json) — includes subject, sender, risk score, category, and explanation for display. Overwritten on every pipeline run; not retained between sessions.
  • LLM explanation cache (llm_cache.json) — stores Gemini plain-text explanations to avoid redundant API calls. Contains no email content; keys are MD5 hashes of email ID, score, subject, and prompt version.
  • Audit log (audit-log.jsonl) — records only decision metadata: timestamp, email ID, score, category, and confidence. No subject, sender, body, or PII.
  • OAuth token (token.json) — stored locally on your device and excluded from version control. Never transmitted to our servers.

3. Data Retention

results.json is overwritten on every pipeline run and is not retained between sessions. llm_cache.json persists across sessions but contains no email content. The audit log is PII-free and may be retained for debugging.

You can request deletion of any cached data by contacting us at harishbabu2510@gmail.com.

4. Third-Party Data Sharing

Email content is sent to the Google Gemini API for deep threat analysis, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No email data is shared with any other third party.

5. Google API Services — Limited Use

Inbox Guardian's use of Gmail data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely for email threat analysis and is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred to third parties.

6. Security

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally and never transmitted to our servers.
  • Email bodies are sandboxed inside <EMAIL_BODY>...</EMAIL_BODY> tags in LLM prompts to prevent prompt injection.
  • The application has no write access to Gmail — it cannot send, delete, or modify emails.

7. Your Rights

Revoke Inbox Guardian's access at any time via Google Account Permissions. To request deletion of cached data, email harishbabu2510@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
